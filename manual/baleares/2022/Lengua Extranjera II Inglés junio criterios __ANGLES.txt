MODEL 3 . OPCIÓ A: “Who is TikTok’s masked ‘vigilante’?”

1. TRUE or FALSE statements (1 point).

a)   TRUE   (0.5) :

‘ The game is online trolling, and the prize is that the Great Londini tracks you down ’ .

b) FALSE (0.5)

‘ But it is something TikTok is not so happy about. The Great Londini has had nine
accounts permanently deleted, and the tenth has been suspended several times ’ .

2. Comprehension question (1 point):

Ideas to be reflected:

∑   Fandom comes at a price; A lot of TikTok followers are actually bullying
people on the platform.

∑   TikTok supporters end up becoming bullies themselves.

3. Vocabulary (1 point):

1)   threat   (0.25);   2)   (To) tell on (0.25);   3)   distorted (0.25);   4)   keen   (0.25).

4. Grammar (2 points):

4.1.   A: has happened/happened
B: has been arrested/was arrested/got arrested   (0.5)

4.2.   a) complaints   b) fearful/fearsome   (0.5)

4.3.   My older brother, who is a lawyer, might be able to help you.

OR   My older brother is a lawyer who/that might be able to help you.   (0.5)

4.4.   She told me not to open the door to anyone.   (0.5)

5. Phonetics and phonology (1 point):

5.1. A)   br a   (0.25)

5.2. B )   sch eme   (0.25)

5.3. C)   ba th   (0.25)

5.4. A)   sav ed   (0.25)

6. Composition (up to 4 points)

Essay compositions will be penalised   if any of the TWO questions   is not specifically
answered and   fully developed . No marks will be given for off-topic essays.

MODEL 3.   OPCIÓ B : “ 'A final of Herculean proportions' - reaction to Rafael
Nadal's epic Australian Open triumph ”

1. TRUE or FALSE statements (1 point).

a)   TRUE   (0.5) :

‘ It came after an exhausting five hours and 24 minutes of nerve-shredding action,
which made it the second- longest major final in history’ .

b)   TRUE   (0.5) :

‘What a match’. ‘Your incredible work ethic, dedication and fighting spirit   are an
inspiration to me and countless others around the world’.

2. Comprehension question (1 point )

Ideas to be reflected:

∑   Nadal struggled to overcome a foot problem last year. He   wasn’t sure   his
foot would be strong enough to be able to play the Australian Open final or
even continue his career.

∑   He also got Covid before Christmas.

3. Vocabulary (1 point):

1)   (To) sum up (0.25);   2)   praise (0.25);   3)   stunning/amazing/impressive (0.25);   4)

former (0.25).

4. Grammar (2 points):

4.1.   A: did (your team) reach   //   has/have (your team) reached?
B: were beaten   (0.5)

4.2.   a) impolite   b) misunderstanding   (0.5)

4.3.   He must be exhausted after being playing for more than
four hours   (0.5)

4.4 . If   Jane hadn’t had an injury on her leg, she could have played
her best game last Friday’ .   (0.5)

5. Phonetics and phonology (1 point):

5.1.   B) a re   (0.25)

5.2.   D) g eneral   (0.25)

5.3.   C) gh ost   (0.25)

5.4.   B)   play ed   (0.25)

6. Composition (up to 4 points)   Essay compositions will be penalised   if any of
the TWO questions   is not specifically answered and   fully developed . No marks will
be given for off-topic essays.
