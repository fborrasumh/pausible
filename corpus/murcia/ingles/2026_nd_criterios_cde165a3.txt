5. Complete THREE of the following sentences so that each sentence keeps the meaning of the
sentence printed before it and includes a clear change of linguistic structure. COPY THE
COMPLETE SENTENCES (including the beginnings already given to you) onto your answer sheet.
[3x0.5=1.5 marks]

   ➔ 0.5 marks for a completely correct answer (i.e., the candidate changes the structure in the
       sentence given and demonstrates accurate knowledge of and ability to use the L2 form(s)
       assessed in the item).

   ➔ 0.25 marks for a partially correct answer (i.e., the candidate has attempted to use the L2
       form(s) assessed in the item, but with some inaccuracy/ies).

   ➔ 0 marks for an incorrect answer (i.e., it is clear that the candidate does not know the L2 forms

SECTION III: WRITING [4 marks / 4 puntos]
 6. Choose ONE of the two options below (6.1 OR 6.2) and write the corresponding text (150-175
 words). Write your answer on your answer sheet. [1x4=4 marks]

 a) Criteria:

   ➔ Content relevance and organization (CR&O): 1.5 marks for answers containing
       between175 and 150 words; 1.2 marks for answers containing between 150 and 120 words;
       0.8 marks for answers shorter than 120 words)

   ➔ Use of English (UoE) (i.e., grammar & vocab): 2 marks for answers containing between 175
       and 150 words; 1.5 marks for answers containing between 150 and 120 words; 1 mark for
       answers shorter than 120 words)
   ➔ Mechanics (Mech) (i.e., spelling, punctuation, and capitalization): 0.5 marks for answers
       containing between 175 and 150 words; 0.3 marks for answers containing between 150 and
       120 words; 0.2 marks for answers shorter than 120 words)

b) Summary of maximum marks granted for the three criteria (a, b, and c) for each type of answer:

   ➔ Between 175 and 150 words: 4 marks (1.5 for CR&O + 2 for UoE + 0.5 for Mech)

   ➔ Between 150 and 120 words: 3 marks (1.2 for CR&O + 1.5 for UoE + 0.3 for Mech)

   ➔ Fewer than 120 words: 2 marks (0.8 for CR&O + 1 for UoE + 0.2 for Mech)

c) Summary of maximum scores for each block of the scoring rubric for each of the three scoring
possibilities indicated above (175-150 / 150-120 / fewer than 120 words):

   ➔ 175 - 150 words / 4 marks:

   ▪   CR&O: 1.5 marks
   ▪   UoE: 2 marks
   ▪   Mech: 0.5 marks

   ➔ 150 - 120 words / 3 marks:

   ▪   CR&O: 1.2 marks
   ▪   UoE: 1.5 marks
   ▪   Mech: 0.3 marks

   ➔ Fewer than 120 words / 2 marks:

   ▪   CR&O: 0.8 marks
   ▪   UoE: 1 mark
   ▪   Mech: 0.2 marks

IMPORTANTE NOTE 1: Exams will be assessed following the criteria in the updated versions of the scoring
rubrics.

IMPORTANT NOTE 2: Answers longer than 175 words will be corrected and marked normally up to the first
end-of-sentence after the 175 words. The exceeding part of the answer will not be considered by the rater for
UoE or Mechs (i.e., no penalization regarding these two criteria will be applied). Raters will, however,
consider the whole text (inc. the exceeding part) in their assessment of the CR&O criterion.

IMPORTANT NOTE 3: If the candidate makes the same (grammatical, lexical, or mechanical) error two or
more times, it will only be penalized one time.

IMPORTANT NOTE 4: Maximum penalization for spelling errors across the exam: 1 mark (10% of max.
exam score).
