NOTE 1: This rubric is to be used with answers which meet the requirements of
Task 6 in terms of number of words (150-175 words). For answers between 150-120
words and answers shorter than 120 words, follow correction and assessment
instructions in “Criterios de corrección”.

NOTE 2: What is a lexical error (in this rubric)? A lexical error involves the
wrong selection of a word or expression (phrasal verb/collocation/proverb). Here are
some examples:

 a)Wrong word (single-word): Juana and Pedro are my sisters siblings.
 b)Wrong phrasal verb (multi-word): My mother wants me to get off get up at 7
 pm every day. (It’s an error if the phrasal verb is entirely incorrect or if the
 main verb or the particle(s) are incorrect.)
 c)Wrong collocation (multi-word): 1) The audience gave the pianist a big ring
 round of applause. 2) He has a purple black eye because he told too many lies.
 d)Wrong proverb or formulaic expression (e.g., as sick as a cat dog; as ill
 sick as a dog; so as sick as a dog; to at any rate; at no any rate; a stitch in
 time makes saves nine, etc.).

Please, note that choosing the right word and an incorrect ending (e.g., plural
morpheme, suffix) or an incorrect prefix is not considered a lexical error but a
grammatical one. For example, if the writer means “injusto” and writes “infair”
instead of “unfair”, this error will be considered a grammatical error, not a
lexical one.

Note too that prepositions errors are also considered grammatical errors, not
lexical errors.

NOTE 3: Note that one thing is to establish and detail the content of the
communication with the recipient (“body” criterion in the rubric), i.e., to do it
or not to do it, and a different thing is whether the content of the
communication with the recipient established in the candidate’s answer is or not
relevant (i.e., directly related to the specific requirements of the writing
prompt). In this section (“body”), what is assessed is whether that bit of
information is included or not, not whether it is correct from an informational
point of view (which is assessed in the “content relevance” criterion of the
rubric).

NOTE 4: In Task 6, two or more instances of the same grammatical, lexical, or
mechanical error will be penalized only once. The same error of either category
(grammatical, lexical, or mechanical) will not be penalized.
