selection of one word or expression (phrasal verb/collocation/proverb). Here are some



                                                   g
examples:




                                               I n
 a)Wrong word (single-word): Juana and Pedro are my sisters siblings.
 b)Wrong phrasal verb: My mother wants me to get off get up at 7 pm every day.



                                      U
 (It’s an error if the phrasal verb is entirely incorrect or if the main verb or
 the particle(s) are incorrect.)


                                    A
 c)Wrong collocation: 1) The audience gave the pianist a big ring round of applause.


                               P
 2) He has a purple black eye because he told too many lies.
 d)Wrong proverb or formulaic expression (e.g., as sick as a cat dog; as ill sick
 as a dog; so as sick as a dog; to at any rate; at no any rate; a stitch in time
 makes saves nine, etc.).

Please, note that choosing the right word and an incorrect ending (e.g., plural
morpheme, suffix), or an incorrect prefix is not considered a lexical error but a
grammatical one. For example, if the writer means “injusto” and writes “infair”
instead of “unfair”, this error will be considered a grammatical error, not a
lexical one.

Note too that prepositions errors are also considered grammatical errors, not
lexical errors.

NOTE 4: In Task 6, two or more instances of the same grammatical, lexical, or
mechanical error will be penalized only once. The same error of either category
(grammatical, lexical, or mechanical) will not be penalized.
