                      Estructura del examen - ALUMNADO GENERAL
SECTION I. READING COMPREHENSION
-   Tarea 1 ➔ 2 marks. Reading comprehension:                                            Choose from:
                - Text length: approx. 350 words                                        Text A (+ 1 & 2)
                - Task:
                       a) multiple choice (4 items) OR                                        OR
                       b) T/F (with evidence of comprehension) (4 items)                Text B (+ 1 & 2)
-   Tarea 2 ➔ 1 marks. Reading comprehension and linguistic accuracy. Task: Sentence completion (2 items)

SECTION II. GENERAL LANGUAGE ABILITY (USE OF ENGLISH)
-   Tarea 3 ➔ 1.5 marks: General language ability. Task: Multiple Choice Cloze (15 items)

-   Tarea 4 ➔ 1.5 marks: Linguistic accuracy. Task: Sentence transformation/completion (3 items)

SECTION III. WRITING
-   Tarea 5 ➔ 4 marks. Writing (1 item; 150-175 words). Task:
           a) Opinion text OR
           b) Argumentative text OR
           c) Descriptive text OR
           d) Personal communication: informal email (accordingly structured)
