                      Estructura del examen - ALUMNADO GENERAL
SECTION I. READING COMPREHENSION
-   Tarea 1 ➔ 2 marks. Reading comprehension:
                - Text length: approx. 300-350 words
                - Task:
                       a) multiple choice (4 items) OR
                       b) T/F (with evidence of comprehension) (4 items)
-   Tarea 2 ➔ 1 marks. Reading comprehension and linguistic accuracy. Task: Sentence completion (2 items)

SECTION II. GENERAL LANGUAGE ABILITY (USE OF ENGLISH)
-   Tarea 3 ➔ 1.5 marks: General language ability. Task: Multiple Choice Cloze (15 items)

-   Tarea 4 ➔ 1.5 marks: Linguistic accuracy. Task: Sentence transformation (3 items)

SECTION III. WRITING
-   Tarea 5 ➔ 4 marks. Writing (1 item; 150-175 words). Task:
           a) Opinion text OR
           b) Argumentative text OR
           c) Descriptive text OR
           d) Personal communication: informal email (accordingly structured)

 Criterios de corrección y evaluación (Task 4 only)
- 0.5 marks for a completely correct answer (i.e., the candidate demonstrates
  accurate knowledge of and ability to use the L2 form(s) assessed in the item):
    E.g.: Amazon will deliver the package tomorrow afternoon.
    The packaged _______________ Amazon tomorrow afternoon.
    ➔ The package will be delivered by Amazon tomorrow afternoon.
- 0.25 marks for a partially correct answer (i.e., the candidate has attempted to
  use the L2 form(s) assessed in the item, but with some inaccuracy/ies):
    ➔ The package will be delivered Amazon tomorrow afternoon.
    ➔ The package was delivered by Amazon tomorrow afternoon.
    ➔ The package will be delivered by Amazon tomorrow afternoon. (Etc.)
- 0 marks for an incorrect answer (i.e., it is clear that the candidate does no
  know the L2 forms assessed in the item or does not know how to use them
  correctly):
    ➔ The package was sent by Amazon tomorrow afternoon. (Etc.)
