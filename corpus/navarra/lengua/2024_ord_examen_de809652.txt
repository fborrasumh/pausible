GUNS IN THE USA

Of the many words synonymous with the USA (freedom, wealth, American
dream), gun violence, unfortunately, is one of them. In the year 2022 alone, there were
over 45 thousand gun-related deaths on American soil. Over 6,000 children were killed
or injured by gunfire and either intentional or indirect use of guns. Gun violence has
recently surpassed car accidents as the greatest threat to American youngsters in causes 5
of death. Suicide by guns has also reached an almost historic peak, with an estimated
7.5 gun suicides per 100,000 people. Incidents like the mass shootings in Dallas, Texas;
Nashville, Tennessee; Buffalo, New York, and Uvalde, Texas, continue to have cities
in mourning and have sparked repeated calls for stricter laws to control gun ownership.

In response to these concerns, President Joe Biden is trying to restrict access to 10
guns. However, the laws and policies are almost impossible to change. The Second
Amendment of the US Constitution states that the right of the people to bear and keep
arms has to be respected. This amendment was written after the American Revolution
to make sure that if the American government turned corrupt and infringed on the
fundamental human rights of the Americans, the people would have the power and 15
weapons to overthrow the government and fight for their own rights. On a deeper level,
gun restrictions are very hard to implement due to the inherent mindset of US citizens;
that of freedom. The government cannot violate human liberties, and one of those
liberties is the ownership of guns.

Besides, although the majority of Americans believe that gun violence is a 20
significant problem in society, the American population is evenly divided into whether
gun ownership increases or decreases safety in America. About half say it helps to
increase safety by allowing citizens to protect themselves, but an equal share says gun
ownership reduces safety by giving too many people access to firearms and,
consequently, by increasing gun misuse. According to the Pew Research Center, 25
personal protection (72%) tops the list of reasons gun owners give for owning a firearm.
Considerably smaller shares say that a major reason they own a gun is for hunting, for
sport shooting, as part of a gun collection or for their job.

CHATGPT

ChatGPT is a conversational artificial intelligence (AI) from the research
laboratory OpenAl. It can assist with various tasks, from answering questions to writing
content. It’s like having a bright friend you can chat with anytime and anywhere! You
can ask questions on many topics, and it can even help you write creative stories. But
here’s where it gets interesting. ChatGPT has become a common resource for academic 5
help, with 30% of college students using the platform for schoolwork. Students find the
app handy for things like brainstorming essay topics or getting clarification on complex
subjects. Using AI tools like ChatGPT for essays eliminates the need to spend hours
researching or drafting. It checks data from countless sources — meaning the
information is usually comprehensive and well-articulated — which produces well- 10
written content that often rivals human-generated work. ChatGPT can generate content
quickly, letting students focus on other tasks or subjects.

In light of this, teachers are sounding the alarm. They’re concerned that tools like
ChatGPT might make it easier for students to submit work that isn’t their own. Many
teachers worry students might use the application to generate essays, potentially 15
undermining the value of original work and academic integrity. Teachers want to assess
students’ understanding and skills, not a machine’s. To address this, schools are using
advanced plagiarism checkers and software designed to detect content generated by Al.
Believe me, they’re getting good at it and if you get caught, you could face severe
consequences. 20

Here’s my advice — if you’re tempted to use ChatGPT to write your academic
paper, think twice. The risks far outweigh the benefits. However, ChatGPT can be a
helpful sidekick if used responsibly. For instance, you can use it to brainstorm ideas,
summarize complex articles or generate an outline. You can blend technology with your
original thoughts. Let ChatGPT help with the groundwork, but make sure the final essay 25
screams “you.” I advise using AI as a jumping-off point, but never letting it do all the
talking. Be intelligent and strategic. Use these tools to your advantage, but remember,
your unique voice and perspective are irreplaceable. Academic success comes from
knowing when and how to use resources without sacrificing integrity.
