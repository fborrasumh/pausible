TEXTO A: Black or black? White or white?

QUESTIONS. Do not copy literally from the text. (1 punto por respuesta = 4 puntos)

1. According to this newspaper, the reason for capitalizing initial ‘B’ in ‘black’ is related to the
existence of a culture and history particularly shared by black people, which is not the case with
either white or brown people.

2. The text includes up to six different wordings or expressions: ‘black’ and ‘negro’ (either with
initial small or capital letter), ‘African-American’ and ‘coloured’. Excepting ‘coloured’, the rest can
appear either in its singular or its plural form, too. So, eleven ways in all.

3. The use of the expresion ‘Blacks’ is closely related to Africa (African history and culture),
inasmuch as the use of ‘Whites’ has a direct relationship with European and Middle-Eastern
history and culture.

4. The US has become a multi-racial nation. The attempt to favour the spelling ‘Black’ is part of a
campaign to make people aware of how much black people have suffered from racism and the
need to correct a historic injustice.

5. Fill in the gaps with the correct, most appropriate word in each case. (0,25 por acierto = 1
punto)

   A) have
   B) are
   C) particular
   D) for

6.A. Rephrase the following sentences so that their meaning is as similar to the original as
possible. (0,25 por acierto = 1 punto)

   a) An apple a day is thought to be good for you.
   b) If you press this button, the machine stops/will stop. // If you want the machine to stop (to
   stop the machine), (you have to) press this button.
   c) The inspector accused Colin of murdering Lord Digby.
   d) As it was cold, I decided to wear two pullovers.

7.A. Write a composition about the following topic (100-120 words) (4 puntos): Pros and cons of
being a black teenager living in a community of white people.

TEXTO B: Face masks

QUESTIONS. Do not copy literally from the text. (1 punto por respuesta = 4 puntos)

1. The coronavirus spreads through respiratory droplets travelling in the air when someone
coughs, sneezes, talks, shouts or sings. Face masks protect other people from getting those
infected droplets into their healthy bodies.

2. The answer to this question is an absolute yes! Researchers claim that face masks should be
worn by everyone. Healthy people wearing face masks have less chance of getting the virus and,
even if they do eventually get it, the effects of the disease won’t be so strong.

3. No, they don’t. Researchers indicate that around 40% of those who have been infected with
the virus do not develop any symptoms. In other words, you can be sick without having
symptoms.

4. Everybody’s wearing face masks not only reduces the rates of infection but also the number of
people hospitalized and the number of people dying from the disease.
