TEXTO A: How COVID-19 affects household spending

QUESTIONS. Do not copy literally from the text. (1 punto por respuesta = 4 puntos)

1. The author of the text would spend at least £50 on takeaway coffees at £3 each, lunches at
£10, glasses of wine at £9 in fancy bars, along with the cost of train and tube tickets, and the
occasional taxi.

2. The author of the text does not travel any more, does not buy any clothes, and is happy with
toast for lunch.

3. Those who have lost their jobs have had to start shopping in cheaper supermarkets, make
things last longer, take their cars off the road to avoid paying taxes, and cut back on their
children’s regular sports club activities.

4. They are now less consumerist and shop locally, they are more careful with spending and no
longer spend unexpectedly, they have reduced travel expenses and what they used to spend on
socializing after work or during lunchtime.

5. Fill in the gaps with the correct, most appropriate word in each case. (0,25 por acierto = 1
punto)
   A) lot
   B) than
   C) has
   D) between

6.A. Rephrase the following sentences so that their meaning is as similar to the original as
possible. (0,25 por acierto = 1 punto)
   a) Tom asked Jill how long she had had / has had her car.
   b) She didn’t remember to bring her keys.
   c) For a long time people believed that the earth was flat / believed the earth to be flat.
   d) Jim must have left when they arrived.

7.A. Write a composition about the following topic (100-120 words) (4 puntos): Pros and cons of
saving money.

TEXTO B: The history of table manners

QUESTIONS. Do not copy literally from the text. (1 punto por respuesta = 4 puntos)

1. The painting shows two children seated at a table ready to eat, using silver cutlery under the
supervision of a maid. The table, covered with a tablecloth, is laid in the kitchen of a large house,
with pots and pans hanging elegantly on the wall. There are also two stately chairs.

2. 2022 marks 161 years since Mrs Beeton’s Book of Household Management was originally
published in 1861, a guide to the fascinating world of table manners.

3. The painting is considered a representation of the front line of a cultural revolution because it
depicts innovative aspects of table etiquette such as using cutlery instead of eating with fingers,
or sitting upright in a high-backed chair.

4. Today barbecued wings and legs are eaten with your hands, and foods such as hamburgers
and pizza are often eaten in front of the TV (presumably not sitting at a table).

5. Fill in the gaps with the correct, most appropriate word in each case. (0,25 por acierto = 1
punto)
   A) to
   B) on
   C) prepared / ready
   D) too

6.B. Rephrase the following sentences so that their meaning is as similar to the original as
possible. (0,25 por acierto = 1 punto)
   a) We weren’t able to solve that problem because it was very difficult / because of its difficulty.
   b) Would you rather have dinner at home or in a restaurant?
   c) This is the first time we have visited Madrid.
   d) An enormous amount of money is spent on beer.

7.B. Write a composition about the following topic (100-120 words) (4 puntos): Describe a family
meal on a festive day of celebration.
